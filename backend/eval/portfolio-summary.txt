THE INTERVIEW ANSWER
═══════════════════════════════════════

"We built a three-dataset eval framework for the AI companion
and ran it across three iterations to drive measurable
improvements. Dataset 1 tested static soccer knowledge against
75 human-written reference answers — 81% factual accuracy and
93% relevance in our final run. Dataset 2b tested our derived
signals engine with 10/10 scenarios passing. Dataset 3 measured
signal impact on live match responses. After identifying three
root causes in Run 2 — phantom judge errors, a factual error
in a test scenario, and intensity signals returning 'low' during
situationally dramatic moments — we fixed all three and saw a
+16pt swing in signal lift, from -8pts to +8pts. The eval
framework didn't just measure quality; it told us exactly what
to fix and proved the fix worked."
